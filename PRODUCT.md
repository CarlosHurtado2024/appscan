# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite, React and TypeScript, published as a PWA on GitHub Pages. Delegated to Claude and approved by the user in the design spec.

## Users

A person and their family, on their own phones. They need to turn paper (invoices, contracts, ID cards, school and medical papers) into a clean PDF, usually standing or sitting at a table, in whatever indoor light is available, often in a hurry to send it to someone.

## Product Purpose

AppScan turns phone photos of documents into straight, legible PDFs that can be shared at once. Success: a sheet on a table becomes a shared PDF in under a minute, with no account.

## Positioning

Everything happens on the phone. No account, no server, no upload: documents leave the device only when the user shares them.

## Operating Context

One-handed phone use. Paper on a desk or table, mixed indoor lighting. Works offline after first use. Interface language is Spanish.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Camera and gallery import, edge detection and manual crop, filters, multi-page documents, PDF export and share, local library, OCR in Spanish, ID card sheet, signatures and annotations, merging and reordering. No sync or backup: losing the device or browser data loses the documents, and the app must say so.

## Brand Commitments

The name is AppScan. Must not resemble CamScanner in name, icon, copy or visual design.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, user counts, or benchmarks exist; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

- The paper is the subject: the interface gets out of the way of the document.
- Never block the task: when automation fails, a manual path is always ready.
- Never lose a capture.
- Say plainly where documents live and what is not backed up.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Touch targets of at least 44 px. Usable with one hand. Text contrast of at least 4.5:1 in light and dark.
